# RandoWand (Fabric)

RandoWand is a lightweight building utility mod for Fabric 1.21.1 designed to streamline texturing, palette blending, and building gradients in Minecraft.

---

## How It Works

* **Randomized Placement:** Load blocks directly into the wand to randomly select and place them as you build.
* **Effortless Gradients:** Eliminate the hassle of manually cycling through hotbar slots when trying to achieve organic, natural-looking block distributions.
* **Simple & Lightweight:** Designed with clean, straightforward mechanics that integrate seamlessly into your building workflow without unneeded complexity.

---

## Crafting Recipe

To craft the RandoWand, place:
* 1 **Diamond** in the top-right corner
* 1 **Iron Ingot** directly underneath the diamond
* 1 **Gold Ingot** to the left of the diamond
* 2 **Sticks** placed diagonally down to the bottom-left corner
